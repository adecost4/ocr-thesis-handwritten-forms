| Aspect                                              | CRNN                                                                                                                                                                                                   | TrOCR                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Visual feature extraction**                       | CNN                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Pretrained Image Transformer (BEiT/DeiT encoder)                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Sequence modeling**                               | Bidirectional LSTM                                                                                                                                                                                     | Transformer decoder with self-attention and cross-attention                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Alignment / decoding**                            | CTC transcription layer                                                                                                                                                                                | Autoregressive sequence generation (WordPiece tokens)                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Character segmentation**                          | Not required                                                                                                                                                                                           | Not required                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Pretraining**                                     | Original paper trains from scratch on synthetic data; does not rely on pretrained vision or language models                                                                                            | Core contribution. Uses pretrained image Transformer and pretrained language Transformer before OCR fine-tuning                                                                                                                          |
| **Architecture era**                                | CNN/RNN (Deep Learning era, ~2015)                                                                                                                                                                     | Transformer (Foundation Model era, ~2021–2023)                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Model complexity**                                | Moderate. CNN + BiLSTM + CTC pipeline with ~8.3M parameters in the original model                                                                                                                      | Higher. Encoder–decoder Transformer with Small (62M), Base (334M), and Large (558M) variants                                                                                                                                             |
| **Compute requirements**                            | Relatively low. Faster training and inference, suitable for limited hardware                                                                                                                           | Much higher. Requires large-scale pretraining, more GPU memory, and longer training time                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Strengths**                                       | Lightweight, end-to-end, no character segmentation, works on variable-length text, efficient inference, strong baseline for OCR                                                                        | State-of-the-art accuracy, benefits from pretrained vision and language models, captures long-range dependencies, unified end-to-end architecture, no external language model required                                                   |
| **Weaknesses**                                      | Limited long-range context, sequential BiLSTM limits parallelism, relies on CTC, cannot leverage pretrained language knowledge                                                                         | Large computational cost, larger model size, requires substantial pretraining resources, more difficult to train and deploy                                                                                                              |
| **Relevance to structured forms** | Good baseline for cropped words or text lines in forms. Suitable when computational resources are limited or real-time inference is important. May struggle with noisy handwriting or complex layouts. | Better suited for handwritten form recognition after text regions are cropped. Stronger language modeling and contextual understanding can improve recognition of ambiguous handwriting, making it a stronger candidate for your thesis. |
